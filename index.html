Good to know

Frequently asked questions

How do I place an order?

Tap any Enquire button. WhatsApp opens with the piece and your personalisation asked for — just send it. Prefer talking? Tap Call.

How much does a piece cost?

Every piece is quoted individually, because size, personalisation and delivery all change it. Send us the piece you like on WhatsApp and we will reply with the price and timeline.

Do you ship across India?

Yes, pan-India with tracked courier. We are based in Navsari, Gujarat, and can also dispatch directly to the person you are gifting.

Can I customise colour, name, photo or my own flowers?

Yes. Names, initials, dates, photographs, colour palettes and your own dried flowers are all possible on most pieces. Send references on WhatsApp and we will confirm what works before you pay.

What payment methods do you accept?

UPI and bank transfer. The quote is confirmed on WhatsApp first, and custom pieces need a percentage advance so the work can start.

How do I care for resin?

Wipe with a soft damp cloth and keep it away from direct flame, hot vessels and long sun exposure. Resin is strong but not unbreakable, so avoid dropping or stacking heavy things on it.

What if my piece arrives damaged?

Send a photo within 48 hours of delivery and we will repair or replace it. We pack carefully and share a dispatch photo or video so you know exactly what left the studio.

Still deciding? Ask us anything.

Real replies from the maker, including photos of pieces in progress — and price quotes for anything you like.
